**Adaptation and changes:** Match the published chemical and magnetic measurements by original site identifier/name; select the 25 distinct surface sites with paired values; retain Fe, Mn, Cu, Ni, Zn and Pb as the declared chemical variables and field/laboratory magnetic values as separate columns; leave out the two additional deep-layer chemical rows 64B and 124b; arrange the matched observations in the V34 analysis CSV format. The published article contains more magnetic field observations than the matched surface sample used here. The measurements were made by the original Zagreb researchers; the V34 team did not collect new Zagreb field data.
