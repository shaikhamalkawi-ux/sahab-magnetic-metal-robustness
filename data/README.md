# Public primary analytic datasets

The [complete V34 release](../release/Sahab_V34_GitHub_Complete_Reproducibility_20261012.zip) and [direct CSV files](../release/data/) contain:
- Sahab n=48 accepted source-reconciled analytic records, selected from the historical survey of 56 records (54 identities reconciled).
- Amman–Airport n=44 locked comparative observations. Sample 8 `Fine_LF=246.05` differs from about `224.390243902439` in a separately published reconstruction; see the source crosswalk.
- Zagreb n=25 surface-site records transcribed/adapted from Table 10 and Appendix A of Frančišković-Bilinski et al. (2023), *Minerals* 13(12), 1481, https://doi.org/10.3390/min13121481, **CC BY 4.0**. Two deeper-layer chemical rows are excluded; this is not a new field survey.

The Zagreb file is now redistributed with source attribution. The historical reduced ZIP remains accessible but is not the default full release. No blanket CC BY or MIT licence is assigned to unrelated Sahab/Amman measurements.
