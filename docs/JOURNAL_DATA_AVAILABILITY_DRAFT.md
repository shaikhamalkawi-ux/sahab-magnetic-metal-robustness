# Data Availability Statement — synchronized with complete GitHub V34 release

The source-reconciled Sahab primary analytic records (n = 48), Amman–Airport records (n = 44), 25 matched Zagreb surface observations adapted from the cited published tables, historical source-reconciliation records, code, numerical and split-level results, simulation outputs, and verification manifests are publicly available at https://github.com/shaikhamalkawi-ux/sahab-magnetic-metal-robustness in release/Sahab_V34_GitHub_Complete_Reproducibility_20261012.zip (SHA-256: 4971969d21f1ba09741e2551eba952a21ef2bb1d4cd33334066e45cb7e73cb95). The Zagreb input is an attributed adaptation of Table 10 (chemistry) and Appendix A, Table A1 (field/laboratory susceptibility), from Frančišković-Bilinski et al. (2023), licensed under CC BY 4.0 (https://doi.org/10.3390/min13121481). The original study reports 27 chemical rows including two additional deeper-layer records; only 25 matched surface locations are included in this reanalysis. These are published measurements, not newly collected observations. The Sahab 48-row analysis is a reconciled subset of 56 historical records. Source-specific provenance, rights, and the Amman sample-8 input-definition discrepancy are documented in the repository. No Sahab-specific Zenodo DOI has been assigned.
